## Non-product facts you may rely on (general coffee knowledge, not product claims)

- Espresso is brewed at roughly 9 bar of pressure at the puck; "15 bar" / "20 bar" on a
  box is the pump's maximum rating, not brew pressure.
- Burr grinders produce an even particle size; blade grinders cannot, which is why they
  are unsuitable for espresso.
- Brew water is typically around 195–205 °F.
- A double shot commonly uses ~18 g of coffee in, ~36 g of liquid out, in ~25–30 seconds
  (the "1:2 in about 25–30 seconds" starting point), then you adjust grind from there.
- Scale (limescale) is the main killer of home machines; descaling frequency depends on
  water hardness and the manufacturer's instructions.
- Machine classes: steam-driven (cheapest, low pressure), pump semi-automatic,
  super-automatic (grinds/doses/brews automatically), capsule.
